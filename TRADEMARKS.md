**AuthNest** and **Elvoroz**, together with their logos, are trademarks of Elvoroz. We use
them to tell people who made a product, so that customers are not confused or misled.
This page explains, in plain language, how you can use them and how you can't.

*(We mark them with ™. Where we have registered a mark, we will mark it with ® and say so
here.)*

## You can, without asking

- **Talk about AuthNest truthfully.** Write reviews, tutorials, comparisons, articles,
  videos and social posts that use the name "AuthNest" to refer to our product.
- **Say you use it.** "Login powered by AuthNest", "We use AuthNest for sign-in" and
  "Integrates with AuthNest" are fine, as long as they are true.
- **Link to us** and refer to our packages by their real names, such as
  `@elvoroz/authnest-react`.
- **Use our name in plain text** in the normal way, in the same size and style as the rest
  of your text.

## You can't, without written permission

- Use "AuthNest" or "Elvoroz", or anything confusingly similar (misspellings, added words,
  look-alikes), **in the name of your company, product, service, app, domain name, social media
  handle, npm package, GitHub repository or browser extension**.
- Use our **logos** or brand assets, or make a logo that looks like ours, in your own logo,
  product, app store listing or marketing. Please ask us first even for logo uses that are
  meant to say you are a customer.
- **Suggest that we sponsor, endorse, certify or partner with you**, or that you are official.
  For example, don't call yourself "an official AuthNest partner" or "AuthNest certified".
- Register our marks, or anything similar, as your own trademark, domain or handle.
- Alter our marks, or use them in a way that is misleading, disparaging or unlawful.
- Use our marks for merchandise.

## Third-party sign-in providers

Logos such as Google or GitHub shown in AuthNest belong to those companies and are used
under their brand guidelines. You need to follow their rules when you show them in your own
product.

## Asking for permission, and reporting misuse

If you would like to use our marks in a way that isn't covered above, write to
**customer@elvoroz.com** with what you would like to do and where it will appear. We
try to reply quickly, and we are happy to say yes to reasonable requests.

If you see someone impersonating AuthNest or Elvoroz, or using our marks in a misleading
way, please tell us at the same address.

We may change this policy over time. Permission we give in writing stays valid on the terms
we gave it.
