# Contributing and feedback

Thanks for wanting to help. AuthNest is a proprietary product, so **this repository does not
accept code**. It contains documentation only, and it is the place for your questions,
reports and suggestions.

## What we welcome

- Corrections to the documentation: unclear steps, mistakes, missing details.
- Bug reports and questions about AuthNest and its SDKs.
- Feature requests, and honest feedback on what's confusing.

Use the issue templates in the **Issues** tab. For security problems, please use the private
route in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

## Before you post

- **Never post secrets:** API secret keys, tokens, passwords, `.env` files or personal
  data of your users. Everything here is public. If you post one by mistake, rotate it in
  your dashboard right away, and then edit or delete your post.
- **Don't post anything you don't have the right to share**, and don't include confidential
  information belonging to you or someone else.
- **Be kind.** We will remove content that is abusive, discriminatory, spam or off-topic.

   build on it, in the documentation, the product and elsewhere, without payment or credit.

   anyone else's rights.
2. **If you propose a documentation edit,** you agree that Elvoroz may use it under the
   [documentation license](LICENSE) without further permission, and that we may edit or

3. **Ideas are welcome, but not owed anything.** We may or may not build what you suggest, or
   reply, and posting an idea creates no obligation, payment, partnership or confidentiality
   between us. If you have an invention or idea you want to keep confidential or be paid for,
   don't post it publicly. Email us first at customer@elvoroz.com.
4. **Your posts are public** and permanent copies may exist on GitHub and elsewhere, and
   GitHub's own terms also apply to them.

If you don't agree with this, please don't post a Contribution.
