# Privacy and your data

This page explains, in ordinary language, who owns what and what AuthNest keeps. It is an explanation, not a legal
document. The binding versions are the [Privacy Policy](https://authnest.org/privacy-policy),
[Terms of Service](https://authnest.org/terms) and [Cookie Policy](https://authnest.org/cookies).

## The short version

- **Your users are your users.** The people who sign up on your website belong to you. You decide what to ask them
  for and why. AuthNest handles their data in order to run sign-up, login and account security for you.
- **We keep what the service needs.** Not more.
- **Passwords are never kept as text.** See [Security and trust](SECURITY_AND_TRUST.md).
- **Consent is recorded**, not assumed.

## What AuthNest stores

**About you (the client)**
- Your name, email address and company name, and the addresses of your website and its server.
- Your subscription and billing status. Card numbers are never stored by AuthNest (see below).
- Records of when you accepted the Terms and which version of the Privacy Policy, plus whether you chose to receive
  product news. Product news is a **separate, optional** choice.

**About your users**
- The details your own sign-up form asks for, which **you** control.
- Their account state: email or phone verified, security settings, registered devices and passkeys.
- Security records: sign-in activity, and the devices and network addresses used, so that suspicious activity can be
  detected and so that users can see where they are signed in.

**About administrators**
- Action logs, so that changes to the platform can be traced.

## What we don't do

- We don't store card numbers. Payments are made on the payment provider's own checkout.
- We don't put tokens in web addresses.
- We do not sell your data or your users' data. *(Our Privacy Policy is the binding statement of this.)*

## Control for you and your users

- **You** can see your users and their activity in your dashboard and manage your account settings.
- **Your users** get a hosted account area where they can update their profile, review security settings and see and
  manage their signed-in devices.
- The platform includes tooling for handling data requests and for breach notifications.
- To ask a question or make a request about data, use [contact us](https://authnest.org/contact-us).

## Your website's own obligations

If your users are in regions with privacy laws, you still need your own privacy notice and a lawful reason for
collecting what you collect. AuthNest is a tool that helps, and it does not replace advice from a qualified
professional about your own situation.
