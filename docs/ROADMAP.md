# Roadmap

This page shows what we're working on so you can see where AuthNest is heading. It is a direction and not a promise
of dates. The live version is on the [public roadmap](https://authnest.org/public-roadmap), and everything that ships
is recorded in the [changelog](https://authnest.org/changelog).

## Now

- **Paid plans.** Payments are being switched on so that you can move beyond the Free plan when you need to. Card
  entry happens on the payment provider's own checkout.
- **Helping our first clients.** We are personally helping early users integrate and fixing what they find.

## Next

- **More social providers.** Microsoft, Facebook, LinkedIn, Discord, GitLab and Apple are built. Each will be switched
  on after testing with the real provider.
- **More translated pages.** The core sign-up and login flows are available in six languages; we're extending
  translation to more of the site.
- **More guides and examples** for popular stacks.

## Have a say

The best way to move something up this list is to tell us you need it.
[Open a feature request](../../../issues/new/choose) and describe what you're trying to do.
