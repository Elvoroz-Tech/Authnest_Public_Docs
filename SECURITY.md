# Security policy

Thank you for helping keep AuthNest and its users safe.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report privately by email to **customer@elvoroz.com** with the subject line **"AuthNest security report"**.
Please do not open a public issue for security problems.

A good report includes:
- what you found and where (URL, feature or SDK name and version),
- the steps to reproduce it,
- what an attacker could achieve,
- any proof of concept, kept as small and harmless as possible.

## What to expect

- We aim to acknowledge your report within **3 business days**.
- We will keep you updated while we investigate and fix it, and we will tell you when it is resolved.
- If you would like credit, we are happy to thank you publicly once the issue is fixed.

## Please

- Test only with accounts you own. Do not access, change or delete other people's data.
- Do not run denial-of-service or high-volume automated attacks, and do not use social engineering against our team
  or our clients.
- Give us reasonable time to fix the problem before you disclose it publicly.

We welcome good-faith research that follows these rules.

## Scope

In scope: the AuthNest website and dashboard, the AuthNest API, and the three published SDKs.
Out of scope: third-party services we rely on (report those to the provider), and findings that need physical
access to a device.

For how we protect the platform, and what we do not claim, read [Security and trust](docs/SECURITY_AND_TRUST.md).
